§ 05 · Key Findings

Three Macroeconomic
Inflection Points

Variance and trend analysis surfaced three pivotal moments requiring deeper narrative explanation for stakeholder reporting.

Inflection Point I
2008
Global Financial Crisis — Resilience Divergence
While global GDP contracted sharply, India maintained ~6.7% growth — a sector-level analysis of FDI flows and trade exposure explained the insulation mechanism and subsequent policy response.
Inflection Point II
2016
Demonetisation Shock — Short-Term Contraction
Cross-sector variance analysis detected a statistically significant anomaly in employment and informal-sector proxies coinciding with the November 2016 currency policy. GDP growth dipped to 8.3% from a prior-year peak.
Inflection Point III
2020
COVID-19 Contraction & V-Shaped Recovery — The Sharpest Swing in the Dataset
India recorded a −7.3% GDP growth rate in FY2021 — the most extreme negative value in the 23-year dataset. Variance analysis flagged this as a 4.2σ deviation from trend. The subsequent FY2022 rebound (+8.7%) produced the dataset's largest YoY swing. FDI inflows paradoxically increased during this period, driven by digital-sector investment — surfaced as a notable divergence between GDP and FDI series.

§ 06 · Policy-KPI Correlation

Macroeconomic Events
Mapped to KPI Movement

The analytical report correlated each identified inflection point with its corresponding policy trigger — replicating executive KPI monitoring workflows.

2001 · LIBERALISATION ERA
FDI Policy Reforms — Automatic Route Expansion
Broadening of automatic FDI approval routes correlated with a 34% increase in net inflows over the following 3 years. Trade-to-GDP ratio began ascending from 26% toward 40%.
2008 · GLOBAL SHOCK
GFC & RBI Stimulus — Counter-Cyclical Policy Efficacy
Reserve Bank of India rate cuts and fiscal stimulus package of ₹1.86 lakh crore. Correlation analysis showed a 0.74 coefficient between stimulus timing and employment recovery lag (8 months).
2014 · STRUCTURAL REFORMS
Make in India & FDI Caps Relaxation
FDI inflows grew from $36Bn (2014) to $62Bn (2018). Manufacturing sector employment showed modest lagged response — surfaced as a sector-level divergence in the report.
2020 · PANDEMIC SHOCK
COVID-19 Lockdown & Atma Nirbhar Recovery Package
Largest GDP contraction in dataset. Govt. package of ₹20 lakh crore correlated with Q3 FY2021 recovery initiation. Digital FDI decoupled from GDP — identified as the most analytically novel finding.
2022 · POST-PANDEMIC
Inflation Surge & Monetary Tightening
CPI peaked at 7.8% amid global commodity shocks. RBI repo rate hikes (250bps over 12 months) — correlation with dampened FY2023 growth provided the report's forward-looking risk note.
